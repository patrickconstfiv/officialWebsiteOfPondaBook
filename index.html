Feature walkthrough

Built for spending that changes shape over time.

Scroll through the flow PondaBook is built around: one project, many updates, a reliable history, and a calmer way to stay on top of important plans.

01

One event, many entries, one clear total.

A renovation or wedding rarely fits into a single payment. PondaBook keeps all related spending, additions, and corrections grouped inside the same project.

  • Track purchases, returns, refunds, and extra items together.
  • See current spending beside your maximum budget.
  • Stay focused on the project, not the spreadsheet cleanup.
02

Add changes anytime without breaking the flow.

Last-minute materials, temporary purchases, and surprise adjustments are normal. The app is designed so they still feel organized when they happen.

  • Update the same project as real life moves.
  • Keep each category visible and easy to scan.
  • Reduce friction when plans change midstream.
03

See a full change history you can trust.

Every modification stays on record, which makes the final number easier to understand and easier to explain to the people involved.

  • Review how the project evolved over time.
  • Spot additions and corrections without confusion.
  • Keep a transparent trail for later reference.
04

Export the project when you need to share or archive it.

When the project is done, or when someone else needs a snapshot, PondaBook makes it easy to send the work outward in practical formats.

  • Prepare records for reconciliation and review.
  • Use PDF, Excel, and other export formats.
  • Keep your finished projects tidy and portable.
05

Switch the mood with minimalist themes.

Different projects can carry different energy. PondaBook includes multiple clean themes so each ledger can feel calm, personal, and visually distinct.

  • Choose a style that fits the project atmosphere.
  • Keep the interface polished without getting noisy.
  • Make practical planning feel more considered.
