Feature walkthrough

Built for renovation planning that changes shape over time.

PondaBook is designed around one project, many updates, a reliable history, and a calmer way to stay on top of renovation expenses and remodeling costs.

01

One renovation, many expenses, one clear total.

A remodel rarely fits into a single receipt. PondaBook keeps related renovation spending, additions, corrections, and refunds grouped inside the same project.

  • Track purchases, returns, refunds, and extra items together.
  • See current renovation costs beside your maximum budget.
  • Stay focused on the project, not spreadsheet cleanup.
02

Add budget changes anytime without breaking the flow.

Last-minute materials, contractor adjustments, and surprise fixes are normal during home remodeling. The app keeps them organized when they happen.

  • Update the same renovation project as real life moves.
  • Keep each category visible and easy to scan.
  • Reduce friction when plans change midstream.
03

See a full renovation cost history you can trust.

Every modification stays on record, which makes the final renovation cost easier to understand and easier to explain to family, partners, or clients.

  • Review how the project evolved over time.
  • Spot additions and corrections without confusion.
  • Keep a transparent trail for later reference.
04

Export the project when you need to share or archive it.

When the renovation is done, or when someone else needs a snapshot, PondaBook makes it easy to send the cost record outward in practical formats.

  • Prepare records for reconciliation and review.
  • Use PDF, Excel, and other export formats.
  • Keep finished renovation budgets tidy and portable.
05

Keep renovation planning calm and readable.

Multiple clean themes make each project ledger feel calm, personal, and visually distinct without making cost tracking noisy.

  • Choose a style that fits the project atmosphere.
  • Keep the interface polished and easy to review.
  • Make practical budgeting feel more considered.
